/ Lesson Module 0101

Information Systems and Information Management

Understand how integrated networks of people, hardware, software, data, procedures, and networks collect, process, and distribute crucial enterprise data to support business decisions.

Covers the 6 Core Components, 5 Major Information Systems (TPS, MIS, DSS, EIS, OAS), Information Lifecycles, and Data Quality.

Key Module Highlights

  • 6 Core Components: Hardware, Software, Data, People, Procedures, and Networks.
  • Enterprise Systems: Transaction Processing (TPS), Management Reports (MIS), and Decision Support (DSS).
  • Information Quality: Ensuring high accuracy, completeness, reliability, and timeliness for effective management.

/ Lesson Module 0202

Ethical Decision-Making in Information Technology

Examine how technology professionals analyze complex digital dilemmas, apply moral frameworks, and uphold professional standards concerning security, privacy, and social welfare.

Explores Ethical Frameworks (Utilitarianism, Deontology, Virtue Ethics), Systematic Problem-Solving Approaches, and Professional Judgment.

Key Module Highlights

  • Major Frameworks: Utilitarianism, Deontology, Virtue Ethics, Rights-Based, and Justice & Fairness.
  • Problem-Solving Workflow: Identify problem, gather information, evaluate options, decide, implement & review.
  • Professional Responsibility: Adhering to codes of ethics, social impact awareness, and continuous learning.
